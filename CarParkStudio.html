The number required is worked out from each option's final bay total. Spaces are AS/NZS 2890.6: 2.4 × 5.4 m dedicated space plus a 2.4 × 5.4 m shared area (one shared area serves the spaces either side), bollard 800 mm from the entry end, placed nearest the building entrance.

Paths and crossings10
Walkways line up on the building entrance and run square to the aisles; bays they pass through go. Kerb ramps are 1:8 max and no longer than 1520 mm (AS 1428.1) — one beside every accessible shared area on a walkway, either side of the driveway on the footpath, and at both ends of at-grade crossings. Raised crossings sit at path level with 1.5 m vehicle ramps. A 2.6 m walkway leaves a 1.2 m landing behind a kerb ramp.
Levels and drainage11
The pavement is one graded plane fitted to the ground: no steeper than 1:20 along the bays and 1:16 in any direction (AS/NZS 2890.1), at least the minimum fall, accessible spaces on 1:40 pads. Pits sit on the low edge, each within its grate capacity by the rational method; pipes are sized by Manning at full flow.
Take the rainfall intensity from the ARR 2019 IFD for the site (Brisbane 5-minute, 10 % AEP is roughly 200 mm/h - confirm) and inlet capacities from QUDM or the supplier.
Driveway12
The Table 3.1 category lookup and Table 3.2 widths are indicative - confirm against AS/NZS 2890.1 and override the category if needed. Sight lines use a 5 s gap (desirable) and stopping sight distance (minimum); the frontage road itself is not modelled.

Every aisle end, ring road corner and the entry are simulated both ways (tractrix rear axle, body plus clearance), at the minimum radius up to +6 m and three lane positions. Other parking14
Motorcycles: a car bay becomes two 1.2 × 2.5 m spaces. Bicycles: a car bay becomes a rail corral near the building entrance (AS 2890.3). EV bays sit nearest the entry with a pedestal at the back. The loading bay runs alongside the ring road in the perimeter row. The service vehicle must be able to drive through - spine layouts are flagged for a turning head.
