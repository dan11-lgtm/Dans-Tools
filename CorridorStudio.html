Traffic and trucks (Sec 9.5)
Table drains and cuttings
TMR RPDM Pt 5B s2.13.1: drain base the wider of 1 m or machine width; min grade 0.5 % unlined, 0.2 % lined. TMR Geotechnical Design Standard - Minimum Requirements cl 4.1: a 4 m minimum bench at the top of any single continuous cut over 10 m (every 10 m on reinforced cuts), berm drain at each bench per MRTS04; unreinforced cuts no steeper than 2V:1H. Invert depth and drain batter are project inputs.
Drainage and flood immunity
Paste IFD instead
TMR RPDM Ed 2 Vol 3 Part 5 (Jan 2024) and Part 5B (Aug 2025) replace the withdrawn Road Drainage Manual. The immunity AEP by road class (Part 5 s4.5.1) and freeboard (s4.6) are not built in - enter the project values. Download IFD depths for the site from the Bureau of Meteorology (2016 IFDs) as CSV. Climate uplift per TMR EP170 (not the ARR Data Hub factor). Blockage per ARR Book 6.
Structures & drainage (concept triggers)
Tunnels
Below the cover limit the tunnel is costed as cut-and-cover. Grade limit default is legacy TMR RPDM Ch 23 (3.5 % general, 3 % desirable for long two-way); AGRT Part 2 sets no maximum and asks for 0.5 % minimum. Envelope default is the AGRD03 Table 8.1 open-road benchmark; the project envelope governs. Under a no-go zone only a driven tunnel is allowed. Rates are in card 5.

4Constraints

No-go zones are never crossed. A penalty zone adds its factor x the road's base cost per metre (factor 2 = triple cost there) and is reported as impact. Double-click or Finish to close a polygon.
QLD constraint layers (QSpatial)

          
Queried live from spatial-gis.information.qld.gov.au over the ground model extent (or the termini plus a margin). Regulated vegetation drops Category X. Reloading a layer replaces its zones. Check currency and licence before relying on any layer; flood lines are the 2011 interim mapping, not a flood study.

          
Existing road network

          

          

          
          

          
Tick "Upgrade existing roads" under Run options to include the upgrade option.

          

          

          
The upgrade option follows the existing network from the start to the end, widening in place and realigning only where the existing geometry falls short of the design speed. Where any option runs within the tolerance of an existing road, pavement is costed at the factor shown (rehabilitate and widen), land inside the existing reserve is free, and the works-under-traffic rate in card 5 is added. Existing bridges and pavement condition are not known. Road data: OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL), or import QSpatial / TMR / council road centrelines.
